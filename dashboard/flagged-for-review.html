Effective-date framing differs across official sources Three official framings CMS: states must implement no later than January 1, 2027 (or earlier at state option). CRS Report R48755: effective beginning December 31, 2026, or sooner at state option. DHCS member outreach letter (Aug 2026): the rules will not go into effect until March 2027 (existing members are assessed at six-month renewals, which begin March 1, 2027 in California). These are consistent in practice - coverage months governed by the requirement begin with January 2027, and existing members are first assessed at renewal - but the differing phrasing is flagged for review. Flagged CMS fact sheet on CMS-2454-IFC Compare CRS R48755 (congress.gov) and DHCS MEDIL I26-25 (dhcs.ca.gov).
Caretaker exemption age wording Exemption is for caregivers of a child 13 or younger CMS fact sheet: parents, guardians, caretaker relatives, or family caregivers of a dependent child 13 years of age and under. $580 income-standard wording differs across DHCS materials Income from any source vs. work at a job and earn DHCS MEDIL outreach letter (operative county instruction): have an income from any source of at least $580 (before taxes). DHCS member-facing Medi-Cal Changes page: work at a job and earn at least $580 a month. CMS fact sheet: have monthly income that is not less than the federal minimum wage multiplied by 80 hours. The narrower member-page phrasing conflicts with the MEDIL; the MEDIL county instruction is the operative guidance. Flagged for review. What to do with these

  1. Tax character of Kalshi event contracts — take the contract facts (P&L statements, trade exports) to a CPA or tax attorney before filing; the IRS has not ruled.
  2. Work-rule specifics ($580 standard, trading hours) — ask your county Medi-Cal office in writing, and keep the answer. AB 2161 requires counties to resolve conflicting information with you before taking adverse action.
  3. Official-page wording differences — linked side by side above for manual comparison; the operative county instruction (MEDIL I26-25) is the most specific California document.
  4. 2027 poverty guidelines — republish around January 2027; thresholds on this site will need a refresh for the 2028 coverage year.
