Do hours managing your own rental property count as qualifying work? — blank (see notes) — No official source reviewed answers this. Established pieces: Medicaid work includes self-employment (CMS preamble; ACWDL 25-30); fees for managing others' property are business/SE income (IRS Topic 414); rent from your own property is generally not SE earnings (IRS Schedule E instructions) and rental activity is generally passive for tax-loss purposes (IRS Pub. 527). None of those tax rules defines Medicaid 'work,' and DHCS has not published whether documented self-management hours (leasing, repairs coordination, bookkeeping for your own units) count toward 80 hours, or what proof would be required. Ask the county before relying on these hours; the $580 MAGI-based income month and structured community service remain verified alternatives. What to do with these

  1. Tax character of Kalshi event contracts — take the contract facts (P&L statements, trade exports) to a CPA or tax attorney before filing; the IRS has not ruled.
  2. Work-rule specifics ($580 standard, trading hours) — ask your county Medi-Cal office in writing, and keep the answer. AB 2161 requires counties to resolve conflicting information with you before taking adverse action.
  3. Official-page wording differences — linked side by side above for manual comparison; the operative county instruction (MEDIL I26-25) is the most specific California document.
  4. 2027 poverty guidelines — republish around January 2027; thresholds on this site will need a refresh for the 2028 coverage year.
