Answered in the 2026-10-18 verification pass

  • What added Welf. & Inst. Code §14005.69? SB 164 (Chapter 27, Statutes of 2026, Sec. 75) added it effective June 29, 2026; AB 2161 (Chapter 209, Statutes of 2026) amended it. The Leginfo history note is the controlling statement: consolidated section, SB 164. This corrects the earlier attribution of the section to AB 2161. Recorded as CA-31.
  • Does the $580 route count unearned income? Yes. It is measured by MAGI-based income for the MAGI household (42 CFR 435.552; 42 CFR 435.603), which includes net rental income, interest, and net capital gains — so $0 earned income is not a bar. Only the county’s month-by-month documentation method for trading gains stays open. Recorded as FL-17.
  • Who is excluded? The exclusion list comes from SB 164, amended by AB 2161; the “23” figure on this site is a count of this project’s catalogue of exemption, deemed-compliance, and hardship rows, not an official enumeration. Recorded as OP-9.
  • Citizen vs. green card. Added on request: U.S. citizens keep full Medi-Cal with no premium; LPRs inside the five-year wait move to fee-for-service in January 2027, with a later July 2027 premium step for ages 21–59 who are not pregnant. DHCS immigration-status table. Recorded as MC-19.

What to do with these

  1. Tax character of Kalshi event contracts — take the contract facts (P&L statements, trade exports) to a CPA or tax attorney before filing; the IRS has not ruled.
  2. Work-rule specifics ($580 standard, trading hours) — ask your county Medi-Cal office in writing, and keep the answer. Two protections are independently documented: counties must check every other basis of eligibility before denying or terminating, and existing members have 30 calendar days plus two contact attempts before noncompliance is found (ACWDL 25-30). A separate “resolve conflicting data” duty is not pinned here, because the subdivision cited for it is the sunset clause in the current code display.
  3. Official-page wording differences — linked side by side above for manual comparison; the operative county instruction (MEDIL I26-25) is the most specific California document.
