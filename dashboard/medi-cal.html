Medi-Cal

Medi-Cal is California's Medicaid program. For adults 19–64, the relevant pathway is the ACA expansion (“MAGI New Adult Group”): household MAGI at or below 138% FPL, with no asset test and no premium — and, through December 31, 2026, no work requirement.

How your income is measured

Medi-Cal uses MAGI: adjusted gross income from your Form 1040 plus untaxed foreign income, non-taxable Social Security benefits, and tax-exempt interest (SSI is excluded). For a trader/landlord with no wages, that means the counting unit is essentially:

net rental income (Schedule E) + savings interest + net capital gains from trading (after loss offsets) + any other taxable income, for the whole tax household.

Rental losses and trading losses can push MAGI below the limit in bad years; gains can push it above. If Medi-Cal ends because income rose, the Covered California path takes over — the county or Covered California will evaluate both when you apply. Full details and sources: Income & MAGI.

Illustration only — not an eligibility determination. A one-person household with $12,000 net rental income, $800 interest, and $6,000 net trading gains has MAGI of $18,800 — below the $22,025 limit (2026), so Medi-Cal would be the path; the same household with $15,000 of trading gains would be at $27,800 — above the limit, pointing to Covered California. Verify your own numbers with the county or Covered California.

How to apply

Medi-Cal enrollment is open year-round. DHCS lists four ways to sign up:

  1. Online: BenefitsCal (apply with your county) or Covered California (also screens for Medi-Cal).
  2. By mail: single-stream application (downloadable in many languages from the DHCS apply page).
  3. By phone: call your county Medi-Cal office.
  4. In person: visit a county office — find your county office.

Source: DHCS — Apply for Medi-Cal.

What changes in 2027

  • January 1, 2027: the federal work and community engagement rule takes effect for the expansion adult group — see Work Requirements for the six ways to satisfy it with $0 earned income.
  • January 1, 2027: retroactive coverage shrinks to one month for childless adults (two months for everyone else).
  • March 1, 2027: renewals come every six months for the expansion group. Answer county mail, texts, and emails immediately — DHCS projects most coverage losses will come from missed paperwork.
  • October 1, 2028: copayments for some adults (capped at 5% of household income; none for emergency care, checkups, pregnancy, pediatric, mental health, or community-clinic services).

Source: DHCS — Medi-Cal Changes.

What about your savings and property?

MAGI-based Medi-Cal does not count what you own. DHCS applies asset limits only to non-MAGI groups (people 65+, people with disabilities, nursing home residents, and families over the MAGI income rules). Your savings account and rental property do not count against MAGI Medi-Cal eligibility — only the income they generate counts. (For reference: those non-MAGI asset limits are $130,000 for one person through June 30, 2027, then $21,000 from July 1, 2027, per DHCS.)

Source: DHCS — Medi-Cal Changes (asset limits section).
