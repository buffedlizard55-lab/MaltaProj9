Covered California (Marketplace)

If your MAGI is above 138% FPL, Covered California is the primary path — federal premium tax credits for 100–400% FPL households, California state subsidies up to 200% FPL (2027), and reduced cost-sharing on Silver plans up to 250% FPL. Marketplace coverage has no work or hours test; eligibility runs on household income and access to other coverage.

How the subsidies work

  • Federal premium tax credit (PTC): available to households with income 100–400% FPL who buy through the marketplace and lack affordable other coverage. It caps what you pay for the benchmark Silver plan and can be taken in advance (lowering each month's bill) or at tax time. Because your income is rental + interest + trading gains rather than wages, nothing about the credit changes — MAGI is MAGI.
  • The 400% cliff is back: the enhanced credits that eliminated the cliff (and capped contributions) expired December 31, 2025 and were not extended by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. Above 400% FPL ($63,840 for one person using the 2026 guidelines) there is no federal subsidy — a reason to watch realized trading gains late in the year.
  • California state subsidy: for 2027, $300 million was appropriated and eligibility extends to households up to 200% FPL ($31,920 individual / $66,000 family of four). Covered California projects 500,000+ recipients, with nearly 200,000 people able to choose a $0-premium Silver plan.
  • Cost-sharing reductions (CSR): if you pick a Silver plan and your income is 100–250% FPL, the plan variant is upgraded (Silver 94 / 87 / 73) with lower deductibles and out-of-pocket maximums.
  • Repay the difference: starting with tax year 2026, if your advance credit exceeded what your final income justified, the entire excess is repaid (P.L. 119-21 section 71305 removed the caps). Report income changes promptly.

When you can enroll

  • October 1, 2026 Current members can start renewing or switching plans.
  • November 1, 2026 – January 31, 2027 Open enrollment for 2027 coverage (a June 12, 2026 federal court ruling, City of Columbus v. Kennedy, vacated a shortened window; California reverts to the three-month period).
  • December 31, 2026 Enroll by this date for coverage starting January 1, 2027.
  • January 31, 2027 Last day to enroll for coverage starting February 1, 2027.

Sources: Covered California — Open Enrollment; Covered California Federal Changes Quick Guide.

Medi-Cal, by contrast, is open year-round — and losing Medi-Cal because income rose is itself a qualifying event for marketplace special enrollment.

The California penalty for staying uninsured

California requires residents to have qualifying coverage, claim an exemption, or pay the Individual Shared Responsibility Penalty with their state return: at least $950 per adult (and $475 per dependent child) or 2.5% of gross income over the filing threshold, whichever is more. Exemptions include income below the tax filing threshold, unaffordable coverage, gaps of three months or less, and health care sharing ministry membership. Since Medi-Cal and marketplace plans both count as coverage, the penalty mainly bites people who forgo coverage entirely.
