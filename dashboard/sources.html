Methodology

  1. Single source of truth. Every table on this site is generated from CSV files in data/; each row carries an id, a status (verified, flagged, or unverified), a source name, and a source URL.
  2. Official-source rule. A row may be marked verified only if its source is official (.gov, Covered California, or Kalshi's official help center for Kalshi-specific practice). scripts/validate.py enforces this mechanically and fails the build otherwise.
  3. No inferred values. If a value could not be confirmed from an official source, the value is left blank, the row is marked unverified, and it appears on the Flagged for Review page with an explanation. Personal eligibility is never determined or assumed.
  4. Irregularities are surfaced, not silently resolved. Where official sources phrase the same rule differently (effective dates, the caregiver exemption age, the $580 income standard), both phrasings are cited side by side and flagged.
  5. Dates are kept distinct. The snapshot date, each source's publication date, and future effective dates (January 1, 2027; March 1, 2027; October 1, 2028) are labeled separately everywhere they appear.
  6. Reproducibility. Rebuild with python3 scripts/build.py; the validator (python3 scripts/validate.py) re-checks sourcing, placeholders, and internal links every time.

Verification performed

  • Every data row was written against a document fetched or search-verified during this research session (2026-10-18); source links point to the exact pages used.
  • Key legal facts were cross-checked across at least two independent official sources where possible (e.g., the 80-hour standard and exemptions: CMS fact sheet + DHCS member materials + CRS; the $580 standard: 42 CFR 435.552(f) MAGI-based measurement + Welf. & Inst. Code §14005.69 text (added by SB 164, amended by AB 2161) + DHCS MEDIL member letter; California's any-one-month test: Welf. & Inst. Code 14005.69(d) + ACWDL 25-30 worked examples; trader/rental earned-income treatment: IRS Topics 429/414 + Schedule E instructions). An independent re-verification session on 2026-10-18 re-fetched the controlling regulation (eCFR), the chaptered California statute, both DHCS letters, CMS, IRS, and Covered California pages and corrected the tables/narratives accordingly (see COORDINATION.md).
  • The build validator confirms: all verified rows carry official https source links; unverified rows have blank values; all pages build without placeholders; all internal links resolve; every non-verified row appears on the Flagged for Review page.
  • Known limitation: external URLs were verified at research time; the validator checks their format and domain, not live availability.
