## 3. California's lookback election — the most beneficiary-friendly option

## 4. California's population definition — broader than federal law

| Item | Verified value | Official source |

Federal 42 CFR § 435.551 reaches only the state-plan adult group and certain §1115 MEC demonstrations. | Compare [42 CFR § 435.551 (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/part-435/section-435.551) |

### The decisive California language for someone with $0 earned income

**MEDIL I 26-25 member outreach letter (verbatim, official DHCS):**

> "This rule says that adults, ages 19-64, will need to work, volunteer, go to school, attend a job-training program, **or have an income** to keep Medi-Cal."
>
> "If none of the reasons listed above applies to you when you renew your Medi-Cal, you can meet this rule by **any one or a combination** of the items below:
> • **Have an income from any source of at least $580 (before taxes) in any month since your last renewal.**
>  ○ People whose hours change frequently or who have seasonal jobs can average at least **$580 per month from any source over 6 months**.
> • Work at any kind of job, volunteer, do community service, or attend a job-training program for a combined total of at least **80 hours in any month since your last renewal**.
> • Go to school at least part time in any month since your last renewal.
>  ○ People who go to school less than part time can combine their class and school-activity hours with work, community service, or job training to meet the 80-hour requirement."

Source: [MEDIL I 26-25 (DHCS, Sept 11, 2026)](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/MEDIL-I26-25.pdf)

**ACWDL 25-30 qualifying-activity table (verbatim excerpt, official DHCS):**

> Criteria: "**Income equivalent to at least 80 hours at federal minimum wage (currently $7.25 per hour x 80 hours). Currently, this amount is $580 a month.**"
> Definition: "**An individual's total gross income for a month must meet or exceed this amount (including earned income, unemployment benefits, State Disability Insurance, retirement income, dividends, etc.)**"
>
> Criteria: "**Average monthly income of at least 80 hours at federal minimum wage (currently $580 a month) for individuals with fluctuating income over a six-month period.**"
> Definition: "An individual whose gross income fluctuates has an average monthly income over the preceding 6 months that meets or exceeds this amount."
>
> Criteria: "**At least 80 hours of work**" — Definition: "**Have a job (working at least 80 hours a month) including self-employment.**"

Source: [ACWDL 25-30 (DHCS, Dec 30, 2025)](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/25-30.pdf)

## 6. Consequence of noncompliance in California (official member-facing warning)

> "If you do not meet these rules, you will get a notice in writing telling you what you need to do before your Medi-Cal will end. The notice will also tell you what to do if you do not agree with the decision, including your right to appeal."
>
> "**If you lose Medi-Cal because you did not meet these rules, you cannot get financial help to pay for a health plan through Covered California. That means you would have to pay the full cost of new health insurance.**"
>
> "You can re-apply for Medi-Cal again at any time."

Source: [MEDIL I 26-25 outreach letter (DHCS)](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/MEDIL-I26-25.pdf) — mirrors federal SSA §1902(xx)(7)(B), see [CMCS Informational Bulletin](https://www.medicaid.gov/federal-policy-guidance/downloads/cib12082025.pdf)

## 7. California statutory noncompliance procedure — WIC §14005.69(j)–(k)

| Step | Verified requirement |
|---|---|
| 1 | If a county cannot verify compliance/deemed compliance/exclusion, it **must** provide the **notice of noncompliance** described in subdivision (k). |
| 2 | It must give the individual **30 calendar days beginning on the date the notice is received** to make a satisfactory showing of compliance, of deemed compliance, or that the requirement does not apply because the person is not an "applicable individual." |
| 3 | It must **continue to provide Medi-Cal services during the 30-calendar-day period** if the individual is enrolled. |
| 4 | If no satisfactory showing is made and the individual is not a specified excluded individual, the county must **deny the application or disenroll no later than the end of the month following the month in which the 30-day period ends**. |
| 5 | **Before** denying or disenrolling, the county must (A) determine whether there is **any other basis for eligibility** for Medi-Cal or another insurance affordability program, and (B) provide **written notice and an opportunity for a fair hearing**. |
| 6 | The notice of noncompliance must be on a DHCS-prescribed form and must include: the county worker/call-center name and phone number; how to make a satisfactory showing (and which months will be assessed); how to show the requirement does not apply; the deadline; how to submit information; consequences of noncompliance and of failing to respond; how to reapply; information about short-term hardship events; the right to request a **state hearing**, the procedures and time limits; and the circumstances under which **aid will be continued** if a state hearing is requested. |

Source: [WIC §14005.69(j)–(k) (leginfo)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=WIC&sectionNum=14005.69); AB 2161 renumbers these to subdivisions (k) and (m) — [AB 2161 text](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billTextClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB2161)
