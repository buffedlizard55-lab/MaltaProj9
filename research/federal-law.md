## 3. Who is an "applicable individual" — 42 CFR § 435.551 (verified text)

An applicable individual is an individual who is **not** a specified excluded individual (§ 435.554) and who is:

- (a) Eligible to enroll or enrolled under the State plan under **§ 435.119** (the ACA adult/expansion group); **or**
- (b) Otherwise eligible to enroll or enrolled in a **section 1115(a)(2) demonstration** providing coverage meeting **minimum essential coverage** as defined at § 435.4, and who is:
  1. At least 19 and under 65 years of age;
  2. Not pregnant;
  3. Not entitled to or enrolled for Medicare Part A benefits, or enrolled for Part B benefits; and
  4. Not otherwise eligible to enroll under the State plan.

Source: [42 CFR § 435.551 (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/part-435/section-435.551)

## 4. The seven ways to demonstrate community engagement for a month — 42 CFR § 435.552(a) (verified text)

## 6. How "monthly income" is measured — THE decisive provision for $0-earned-income households

**42 CFR § 435.552(f)(2), verified text:**

> "The agency must determine the monthly income based on the individual's **MAGI-based income, for their MAGI-based household**, as defined at § 435.603, and applied to a month in the period under § 435.556(a), as applicable for demonstrating community engagement."

Source: [42 CFR § 435.552(f) (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/part-435/section-435.552)

**42 CFR § 435.603(e), verified text:**

> "**MAGI-based income** means income calculated using the same financial methodologies used to determine **modified adjusted gross income as defined in section 36B(d)(2)(B) of the Code**," with limited exceptions (lump sums counted only in month received; education scholarships excluded; certain AI/AN income excluded).

Source: [42 CFR § 435.603 (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-C/part-435/subpart-G/section-435.603)

**42 CFR § 435.603(g), verified text:** for MAGI-determined individuals the agency **must not** apply any assets/resources test or income disregards.
Source: [42 CFR § 435.603(g) (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-C/part-435/subpart-G/section-435.603)

**Consequence (this is analysis, not a quote):** MAGI = AGI + tax-exempt interest + excluded foreign earned income + non-taxable Social Security (IRC § 36B(d)(2)(B)).
Official confirmation of the MAGI components: [IRS — Modified adjusted gross income](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/modified-adjusted-gross-income); [HealthCare.gov — MAGI glossary](https://www.healthcare.gov/glossary/modified-adjusted-gross-income-magi/); [HealthCare.gov — What's included as income](https://www.healthcare.gov/income-and-household-information/income/).
AGI includes **rental income (Schedule E), interest (Schedule B), capital gains (Schedule D), and "other income" (Schedule 1, line 8z)**. Therefore the $580/month test is satisfied by **unearned** income, not only by wages.

**CMS preamble confirmation that self-employment counts as "work"** (verified text from the official Federal Register document, section II.C.1 "Work"):

> "At new § 435.552(b), we define work to mean: work in exchange for money, work in exchange for goods or services ('in-kind' work), and unpaid work other than community service … **Many individuals work for themselves by being self-employed** due to starting a business, owning a business, or as an independent contractor, and **these activities meet the definition of work at § 435.552(b)**. Under our definition of work at § 435.552(b), **the hours the individual spends performing these duties would count toward meeting the 80-hour requirement.**"

Source: [Federal Register, 91 FR 33348 (June 3, 2026)](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026-06-03/2026-11094/medicaid-program-community-engagement-requirement-for-certain-individuals) · official PDF: [govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-06-03/pdf/2026-11094.pdf)

**CMS preamble on converting income to hours** (verified text, same document):

> "if the monthly income is less than the applicable Federal minimum wage multiplied by 80 hours and the State does not have information regarding the number of hours worked, States have the option to apply an equivalency standard that allows the State to calculate the number of work hours for a month by **dividing the individual's monthly income by the Federal minimum wage**."

Regulatory text: [42 CFR § 435.552(e)(2)(i)–(ii) (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/part-435/section-435.552)

## 7. Definitions — 42 CFR § 435.552(b) (verified text)

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Work** | "(i) Work in exchange for money; (ii) Work in exchange for goods or services ('in-kind' work); and (iii) Unpaid work (other than community service as defined in this paragraph (b))." |
| **Community service** | "unpaid work, completed voluntarily or because of a mandate by court order, with a **structured program** that is completed for the **direct benefit of the community** under the auspices of **public or nonprofit organizations** (including embedded activities of the program that allow an individual to develop skills necessary to complete community service)." The organization must include 501(c)(3) and other organizations; must provide oversight; the activity must not serve a partisan purpose; and the organization must track type, dates, hours, and have a contact who can confirm hours. |
| **Educational program** | (i) institution of higher education (HEA §101, 20 U.S.C. 1001); (ii) career and technical education program (Perkins Act §3(5), 20 U.S.C. 2302(5)); (iii) a high school (ESEA title VIII, 20 U.S.C. 7801 et seq.); (iv) a State-approved program of study leading to a certificate of high school equivalence for someone without a diploma. |
| **Work program** | (i) WIOA title I program; (ii) Trade Act of 1974 §236 program (19 U.S.C. 2296); (iii) a State/political-subdivision employment & training program meeting Governor-approved standards, including a Food and Nutrition Act §6(d)(4) program — **other than a supervised job search or job search training program** (though those may be subsidiary if < half the required hours); (iv) DOL or VA employment & training program for veterans (must be approved); (v) a workforce partnership under Food and Nutrition Act §6(d)(4)(N). |

Source: [42 CFR § 435.552(b) (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/part-435/section-435.552)

### Education-hour mechanics (§ 435.552(c)–(d))
- Enrollment status (full-time / half-time / less-than-half-time) is **determined by the school or institution**, begins the first day of the term, continues through vacation and recess, and ends at the end of the month the student is expelled, withdraws, completes the term without registering for the next, or graduates.
- **Less-than-half-time**, credit-hour programs: multiply each credit hour of instruction **× 3** to get weekly education hours, then **× 4.33** weeks for monthly hours.
- Non-credit-hour programs: hours spent attending class and participating in educational activities count.

Source: [42 CFR § 435.552(c), (d) (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/part-435/section-435.552)

## 8. Mandatory exceptions — 42 CFR § 435.553 (verified text)

A State **must** deem an applicable individual to have demonstrated community engagement for a month if — for part or all of that month — the individual was:
- (a)(1) Under the age of 19;
- (a)(2) Entitled to or enrolled for Medicare Part A, or enrolled for Part B;
- (a)(3) Described in any **mandatory coverage group** in subclauses (I)–(VII) of section 1902(a)(10)(A)(i); or
- (a)(4) A specified excluded individual under § 435.554; or
- (b) At any point during the **3-month period ending on the first day of that month**, an inmate of a public institution.

Source: [42 CFR § 435.553 (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/part-435/section-435.553)

## 10. Optional short-term hardship exceptions — 42 CFR § 435.555 (verified text)

**At State option.** A State may deem an applicable individual compliant for a month in which, for all or part of the month:

1. The individual **receives inpatient-level services** — inpatient hospital (§440.10), nursing facility (§440.155), ICF/IID (§440.150), inpatient psychiatric hospital services (§440.160, including under-21 without regard to IMD status), or **other services of similar acuity** including inpatient services in a critical access hospital (§440.170(g)), an emergency hospital (§440.170(e)), an institution for mental diseases, other State-recognized non-Medicaid-covered facilities, and **non-institutional services that but for their receipt would likely result in such institutionalization**. *Requires a request from the individual.*
2. The individual **resides in a county** (or equivalent unit of local government) with a **Presidentially declared emergency or disaster** (National Emergencies Act, 50 U.S.C. §1601 et seq.; Stafford Act, 42 U.S.C. §5121 et seq.). *No individual request required.* Duration for Stafford declarations: the first month of the incident period through at least the end of the month it ends, extendable by CMS.
3. The State demonstrates and **CMS determines**, using **U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics** or another reliable source such as a State labor department, that the individual resides in a county whose **unemployment rate is at or above the lesser of 8% or 1.5× the national unemployment rate**. *No individual request required.*
4. The individual, **or the individual's dependent**, must **travel outside their community of residence for an extended period** to receive medical services necessary to treat a **serious or complex medical condition** (as defined at §435.554(c)(5)(i)(E)) **not available within their community**. If the individual does not travel with the dependent, they must show leave from employment or absence from other community engagement for reasons related to the dependent's condition/travel (e.g., taking the dependent to local appointments, travel logistics, being the primary communicator with providers). *Requires a request.*

"Dependent" (§435.555(b)(1)) = the applicable individual's minor child living with them; their **tax dependent** (whether or not a minor child or residing with them); or an individual for whom they have been appointed guardian by a court.
States must provide notice, a request method, timeframes, timely determinations, notice of determination with an anticipated end date, and **appeal rights**. States **must not** require a request for circumstances (2) and (3), and **must not** apply hardship exceptions to specified excluded individuals.

Source: [42 CFR § 435.555 (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/part-435/section-435.555); [CMCS Informational Bulletin, section V](https://www.medicaid.gov/federal-policy-guidance/downloads/cib12082025.pdf)

## 11. Timing of the compliance test — 42 CFR § 435.556 (verified text)

- **New applicants:** must demonstrate community engagement for **at least 1 but not more than 3 consecutive months, as specified in the State plan, immediately preceding the month of application**. (§435.556(a)(1))
- **Existing enrollees:** must demonstrate community engagement for **1 or more months**, as specified in the State plan, **whether or not consecutive**, during the period between the most recent determination/redetermination and the renewal due date (or, at State option, between more frequent verification points). (§435.556(a)(2))
- A State **must not** require demonstration for a period longer than those windows. (§435.556(b))
- CMCS interpretation: a beneficiary meets the requirement if during **any part** of the eligibility period they demonstrated community engagement for the number of months the State specified; **the State may not dictate which specific months**. If more than one month is required, the State must **permit but may not require** consecutive months.

Sources: [42 CFR § 435.556 (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/part-435/section-435.556); [CMCS Informational Bulletin, section VI](https://www.medicaid.gov/federal-policy-guidance/downloads/cib12082025.pdf)

## 12. Verification — 42 CFR § 435.557 (verified text)

- States must **first use "reliable information available to the State"** before requesting anything from the individual. The regulation enumerates: electronic data sources deemed effective and documented in the State's verification plan; information from other State/local agencies; community-engagement information from **Federal agencies and other data sources provided through the electronic service established by the Secretary** (§435.949); information in the State's eligibility system; information in the case record; **payroll data**; claims adjudicated in the preceding 12 months (paid, pended or denied); and **encounter data** for the preceding 12 months.
- Documentation may be required when reliable information is unavailable or **not reasonably compatible** with the individual's statement; before Jan 1, 2028 under §435.952(c), and beginning **January 1, 2028** under a changed standard.
- States must document community-engagement verification policy in their **verification plan under §435.945(j)**.
- Medical-frailty exclusions are carved out of the "reliable information" restriction (subject to §435.557(g)(1)).

Source: [42 CFR § 435.557 (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/part-435/section-435.557)

## 13. Noncompliance — §1902(xx)(6) of the Act (verified from CMCS bulletin)

If a State **cannot establish** that someone met the requirement or was not required to:
1. The State must provide a **notice of noncompliance**;
2. The individual gets **30 calendar days from the date the notice is received** to demonstrate compliance or show the requirement does not apply;
3. The State must **continue to provide medical assistance during the 30-day period**;
4. If no satisfactory showing is made, the State must determine whether the individual has **any other basis** for Medicaid or another insurance affordability program eligibility;
5. The State must provide **written notice and fair hearing rights** under 42 CFR part 431 subpart E (including advance notice for termination), and if there is no other basis for eligibility, **deny the application or terminate eligibility by the end of the month following the 30-day period**.

The CMS fact sheet states the same 30-calendar-day cure period and that the application may be denied or the person disenrolled.

Sources: [CMCS Informational Bulletin, section VII](https://www.medicaid.gov/federal-policy-guidance/downloads/cib12082025.pdf); [CMS fact sheet](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/medicaid-community-engagement-requirement-certain-individuals-interim-final-rule-comment-period-cms); [42 CFR § 435.558 (eCFR)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/part-435/subpart-F/subject-group-ECFRae6babdeed513af)

## 14. The premium-tax-credit trap — §1902(xx)(7)(B) of the Act (verified from CMCS bulletin)

> "An individual who is eligible for Medicaid coverage that provides MEC is generally not eligible for advance payments of the premium tax credit (APTC) and premium tax credits (PTC) used to pay for coverage on a Health Insurance Exchange. **Under section 1902(xx)(7)(B) of the Act, an individual is deemed to be eligible for MEC if the individual would have been eligible for Medicaid but for their failure to meet community engagement requirements.** As such, an applicable individual who does not demonstrate community engagement and would otherwise be eligible for Medicaid is **precluded from eligibility for APTC and PTC**."

Section 1902(xx)(7)(A) separately protects the State's enhanced FMAP: a State is not treated as failing to cover all adult-group individuals solely because one was found ineligible for failure to demonstrate community engagement.

Source: [CMCS Informational Bulletin, section VII](https://www.medicaid.gov/federal-policy-guidance/downloads/cib12082025.pdf)

## 15. Outreach duty — §1902(xx)(8) of the Act (verified from CMCS bulletin)

Before initial implementation and periodically thereafter, States must notify applicable individuals. The notice must explain how to comply, the definition of "applicable individual," the exceptions, the consequences of noncompliance, and how to report a status change. Delivery must be **by mail (or electronic format if elected)** plus **one or more additional formats** (telephone, text message, website, other electronic means).

**Outreach deadline formula:** the number of months the State requires at application **plus three months**, counted back from the implementation date. For January 1, 2027 implementation: **no later than September 2026** (1-month lookback), **August 2026** (2-month), or **July 2026** (3-month).

Source: [CMCS Informational Bulletin, section VIII](https://www.medicaid.gov/federal-policy-guidance/downloads/cib12082025.pdf)

## 16. Managed care limitation — WFTC §71119(c) (verified from CMCS bulletin)

States are **prohibited** from using Medicaid MCOs, PIHPs, PAHPs, or contractors with direct/indirect financial relationships to them **to determine beneficiary compliance** with community engagement requirements. Other support activities may be delegated.

Source: [CMCS Informational Bulletin, section IX](https://www.medicaid.gov/federal-policy-guidance/downloads/cib12082025.pdf)
