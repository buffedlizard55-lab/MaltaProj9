# Coverage Options — U.S. citizen residing in California, property manager & full-time Kalshi trader, $0 earned income

Task: T4 · Owner session: `690e8271` · Verification date: 2026-10-18 (UTC)

**Scope note (assumption, flagged as F-06):** the request describes a "California permanent resident". This
analysis assumes a **U.S. citizen residing in California**. Immigration-status eligibility is a separate
question with its own rules (H.R.1 §71109 narrowed federally funded full-scope Medi-Cal from
**October 1, 2026**, and Covered California limits Marketplace financial help to certain immigrant
groups from **January 1, 2027**). If the person is a lawful permanent resident rather than a citizen,
re-run the eligibility screen against those provisions.

## 2. The compliance pathways, ranked for this specific fact pattern

## 3. ⚠ THE CRITICAL TRAP — losing Medi-Cal for noncompliance also kills the Marketplace subsidy

| Item | Verified text | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Federal rule | SSA §1902(xx)(7)(B): "an individual is **deemed to be eligible for MEC** if the individual would have been eligible for Medicaid **but for their failure to meet community engagement requirements**. As such, an applicable individual who does not demonstrate community engagement and would otherwise be eligible for Medicaid is **precluded from eligibility for APTC and PTC**." | [CMCS Informational Bulletin, Dec 8 2025, section VII](https://www.medicaid.gov/federal-policy-guidance/downloads/cib12082025.pdf) |
| California tells members the same thing in writing | "**If you lose Medi-Cal because you did not meet these rules, you cannot get financial help to pay for a health plan through Covered California. That means you would have to pay the full cost of new health insurance.**" | [MEDIL I 26-25 member outreach letter (DHCS)](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/MEDIL-I26-25.pdf) |
| Why it matters here | Losing Medi-Cal is normally a **qualifying life event** for a Marketplace special enrollment period with subsidies. For a work-requirement loss it is not. The person could end up **uninsured and paying full premium**, plus a **California individual-mandate penalty** for months without MEC. | [FTB — Personal Health Care Mandate](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/personal/filing-situations/health-care-mandate/personal.html) |
| Escape valve | California's county must, **before** denying or disenrolling, "determine whether there is **any other basis** for eligibility for the Medi-Cal program **or for another insurance affordability program**", and must give written notice and a **fair hearing** opportunity. The person may **reapply at any time**. | [WIC §14005.69(j)(5)](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=WIC&sectionNum=14005.69) · [MEDIL I 26-25](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/MEDIL-I26-25.pdf) |
