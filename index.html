Healthcare with no earned income in California

A source-linked guide for a U.S. citizen living in California who manages property and trades Kalshi contracts, with rental income and savings interest but no wages. The key distinction: no earned income does not automatically mean no countable income—and, by itself, does not bar you from Medi-Cal.

Research checked October 18, 2026 · Policy summary, not an eligibility determination or legal/tax advice
Bottom line. If you live in California and your household meets the applicable income rules, apply for Medi-Cal even if you have no wages. California says Medi-Cal applications are accepted year-round, and its joint application also screens for Covered California. Rental income, investment income and capital gains can affect the income calculation. Starting in 2027, a separate federal community-engagement test may apply to some adults in the ACA adult-expansion Medi-Cal group; federal regulation measures its $580 income route with MAGI-based monthly income from any source, and California tests any one month per renewal period—not every month. Losing Medi-Cal for noncompliance does not unlock subsidized Covered California coverage. See who qualifies · See the 2027 rules.
Apply now
Medi-Cal accepts applications all year. You do not need to wait for enrollment season.
≤138% FPL
Most adults qualify for MAGI Medi-Cal at household income up to 138% of the federal poverty level; details depend on household/program facts.
Jan. 1, 2027
Federal community-engagement requirement is scheduled to begin; it applies to a limited adult-group population, not every Medi-Cal member.
$580 / month
MAGI-based income at or above this amount from any source in a qualifying month is one compliance route. It is not an income-eligibility limit.

Income eligibility

“No earned income” is not the same as “zero countable income.”

For Marketplace/MAGI estimates, federal guidance expressly lists net rental income, expected interest/dividends, capital gains, and self-employment net income as income types. The tax treatment and amount of Kalshi contract proceeds are not determined by the fact that you call yourself a full-time trader. Declare accurate estimates and have the county decide Medi-Cal treatment.

Community engagement

Earned income is only one route—and not the only route.

If you are subject to the 2027 requirement and no month clears $580 in MAGI-based income, the alternatives are 80 hours in a qualifying month of work (self-employment included in general), structured community service or a work program, at least half-time education, combinations, and exemptions. Whether hours trading your own account or managing your own rentals qualify is not confirmed anywhere official—do not rely on them without written county confirmation.
