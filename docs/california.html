3. The income test in California — both versions of the statute

Two wordings of WIC §14005.69(e)(6) are in circulation because AB 2161 amended the section. Both are quoted; neither limits the test to earned income. See flagged item F-19.

VersionVerified textSource
As added by SB 164 (Stats. 2026, Ch. 27, §75)§14005.69(e)(6): “The individual has a total monthly income, including, but not limited to, earned income, unemployment benefits, state disability insurance, retirement income, or dividends, that is no less than the applicable minimum wage requirement under Section 206 of Title 29 of the United States Code, multiplied by 80 hours.”CA LegInfo
As amended by AB 2161 (Ch. 209, Stats. 2026)§14005.69(e)(6): “The individual has a total monthly income, using modified adjusted gross income (MAGI)-based financial methods, as specified in Section 1396a(e)(14) of Title 42 of the United States Code, for their individual MAGI-based household budget, that is no less than the applicable minimum wage requirement under Section 206 of Title 29 of the United States Code, multiplied by 80 hours.”CA LegInfo
Seasonal / fluctuating income§14005.69(e)(7) as amended: “The individual had an average monthly income over the preceding six months, based on the individual’s MAGI-based household budget, as described in paragraph (6), that is no less than the applicable minimum wage requirement … multiplied by 80 hours, and is a seasonal worker, as described in Section 45R(d)(5)(B) of the federal Internal Revenue Code of 1986.”CA LegInfoU.S. Code
Effect of the AB 2161 changeAnalysis, not a quotation: the amendment aligns the state test with 42 CFR §435.552(f)(2) (MAGI-based household income). Because MAGI is built from adjusted gross income, unearned income — rent, interest, dividends, capital gains, and “other income” — remains countable under both versions.eCFRIRS

4. What DHCS tells members, in DHCS's own words

This is the most direct official answer to the question “how do I comply with $0 earned income?”

DocumentVerbatim languageSource
MEDIL I 26-25 member outreach letter (Sept 11, 2026)“This rule says that adults, ages 19-64, will need to work, volunteer, go to school, attend a job-training program, or have an income to keep Medi-Cal.”DHCS, Medi-Cal Eligibility Division
MEDIL I 26-25 — the income pathway“you can meet this rule by any one or a combination of the items below: ● Have an income from any source of at least $580 (before taxes) in any month since your last renewal.  ○ People whose hours change frequently or who have seasonal jobs can average at least $580 per month from any source over 6 months.”DHCS, Medi-Cal Eligibility Division
MEDIL I 26-25 — the hours pathways“● Work at any kind of job, volunteer, do community service, or attend a job-training program for a combined total of at least 80 hours in any month since your last renewal. ● Go to school at least part time in any month since your last renewal.  ○ People who go to school less than part time can combine their class and school-activity hours with work, community service, or job training to meet the 80-hour requirement.”DHCS, Medi-Cal Eligibility Division
ACWDL 25-30 qualifying-activity table — incomeCriteria: “Income equivalent to at least 80 hours at federal minimum wage (currently $7.25 per hour x 80 hours). Currently, this amount is $580 a month.” Definition: “An individual’s total gross income for a month must meet or exceed this amount (including earned income, unemployment benefits, State Disability Insurance, retirement income, dividends, etc.)”DHCS
ACWDL 25-30 qualifying-activity table — workCriteria: “At least 80 hours of work” — Definition: “Have a job (working at least 80 hours a month) including self-employment.”DHCS
DHCS Medi-Cal Changes consumer page“Members must do one or more of the following: • Work at a job and earn at least $580 a month • Be a seasonal worker and earn an average of $580 a month over the last six months • Be in a job training program … for at least 80 hours a month • Volunteer or do community service for at least 80 hours a month • Go to school at least half-time (usually two or three classes, or about six to eight credits each semester) • Do a mix of the activities above so the total is at least 80 hours a month or at least $580 a month.”DHCS
DHCS Medi-Cal Changes — the warning“If you are required to meet the rules and do not, you could lose your Medi-Cal.” and “When you get a letter from Medi-Cal or your county office, open it right away and respond quickly.”DHCS
MEDIL I 26-25 — the subsidy trap, in DHCS's words“If you lose Medi-Cal because you did not meet these rules, you cannot get financial help to pay for a health plan through Covered California. That means you would have to pay the full cost of new health insurance.”DHCS, Medi-Cal Eligibility Division
MEDIL I 26-25 — automatic verification“We may be able to use information that we already have about you to determine if you meet one or more of the reasons listed above. If we can do this, you will receive a notice that says you are excused … This notice will also say that you can keep your Medi-Cal and that you do not need to take any further actions. If we do not have enough information to automatically determine … you will have the opportunity to provide information that could excuse you from this rule.”DHCS, Medi-Cal Eligibility Division
