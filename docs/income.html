3. The IRS “trader” test — and why it still does not create earned income

IRS Topic No. 429 sets the official test. A full-time trader may well satisfy it. It does not change the character of the income.

RequirementVerified textSource
All three conditions must be met“To be engaged in business as a trader in securities, you must meet all of the following conditions: • You must seek to profit from daily market movements in the prices of securities and not from dividends, interest, or capital appreciation; • Your activity must be substantial; and • You must carry on the activity with continuity and regularity.”IRS · Topic No. 429
Facts and circumstances considered“Typical holding periods for securities bought and sold; The frequency and dollar amount of your trades during the year; The extent to which you pursue the activity to produce income for a livelihood; and The amount of time you devote to the activity.”IRS · Topic No. 429
Self-labelling does not matter“It doesn’t matter whether you call yourself a trader or a day trader, you’re an investor for Federal income tax purposes.”IRS · Topic No. 429
It is a trade or business“Special rules apply if you’re a trader in securities, in the business of buying and selling securities for your own account. The law considers this to be a trade or business, even though a trader doesn’t maintain an inventory and doesn’t have customers.”IRS · Topic No. 429
Mark-to-market electionTraders may elect §475(f) mark-to-market. Without a valid election, gains and losses are capital and go on Schedule D and Form 8949. With a timely valid election they are generally ordinary and go on Form 4797 Part II. The election must be made “by the due date (not including extensions) of the tax return for the year prior to the year for which you intend the election to become effective.”IRS · Topic No. 429
“Security” for §475(c)(2)“under section 475(c)(2), the term security includes a share of stock, beneficial ownership interests in certain partnerships and trusts, evidence of indebtedness, and certain notional principal contracts, as well as evidence of an interest in, or a derivative financial instrument in, any of these items and certain identified hedges of these items.” Whether CFTC event contracts fall inside this definition is not settled by any official source located in this session — flagged F-04.IRS · Topic No. 429
