Section 71119 of Public Law 119-21 · SSA §1902(xx) · 42 CFR Part 435 Subpart F · California WIC §14005.69 · verified against official primary sources on 2026-10-18

Headline finding. The compliance test is not an earned-income test. 42 CFR §435.552(f)(2) requires the agency to measure monthly income on the individual's MAGI-based income for their MAGI-based household, and MAGI is built from adjusted gross income — which includes rental income, interest and trading gains. California says the same thing to members in plain words: “Have an income from any source of at least $580 (before taxes) in any month since your last renewal.” eCFR · 42 CFR 435.552eCFR · 42 CFR 435.603DHCS/MCED · MEDIL I 26-25

Executive summary

QuestionAnswerWhere to verify
What is the new requirement? Section 71119 of H.R. 1 (Public Law 119-21, enacted July 4, 2025) added subsection (xx) to section 1902 of the Social Security Act. States must condition Medicaid eligibility for “applicable individuals” on demonstrating community engagement. CMS implemented it in CMS-2454-IFC, 91 FR 33348 (June 3, 2026), codified at 42 CFR §§435.550–435.563, effective July 31, 2026, sunsetting October 1, 2034. congress.gov · H.R.1CMS/CMCS · Section 71119govinfo · 91 FR 33348eCFR · 42 CFR Part 435 Subpart F
Who does it apply to? Non-pregnant adults 19 through 64, not entitled to or enrolled in Medicare, who are eligible for or enrolled in the Medicaid adult group (the ACA expansion group) or in certain §1115 demonstrations providing minimum essential coverage. Applies in the 50 states and DC — not the territories. CMS states 43 states and DC must implement. eCFR · 42 CFR 435.551CMS · Fact SheetCMS/CMCS · Section 71119
When? States must implement no later than January 1, 2027, or earlier at state option. California's statute says no sooner than January 1, 2027 and only after DHCS certifies in writing that systems are programmed. DHCS's member letter tells members the rules “will not go into effect until March 2027” — see flagged item F-11. CMS/CMCS · Section 71119CA LegInfo · WIC § 14005.69DHCS/MCED · MEDIL I 26-25
How do you comply with $0 earned income? Use the income pathway: monthly MAGI household income of at least $580 (80 × $7.25). Rental income, savings interest and Kalshi trading gains are all in AGI and therefore in MAGI. In California only one qualifying month since the last renewal is required. Backups: half-time enrolment in an educational program (no hours, no income), 80 hours of community service, or 80 hours in a work program (free through America's Job Center of California). eCFR · 42 CFR 435.552eCFR · 42 CFR 435.603CA LegInfo · WIC § 14005.69DHCS/MCED · MEDIL I 26-25DHCS · ACWDL 25-30
Can trading or property management count as “work” hours? Managing property for third parties for a fee is ordinary self-employment and the hours count — CMS says so expressly. Managing your own rentals is a reasonable reading of the same definition but is not addressed by any official guidance. Kalshi trading is unverified — no CMS, DHCS or IRS guidance addresses it. Both are flagged (F-02, F-03) rather than asserted. Fed. Reg. · 91 FR 33348eCFR · 42 CFR 435.552IRS · Topic No. 429
What is California doing to help? It wrote the requirement into WIC §14005.69 (SB 164, Stats. 2026 Ch. 27) with three safeguards federal law does not require: implementation only after the DHCS Director certifies in writing to the Department of Finance that systems are programmed; implementation only to the extent federal approvals are obtained and federal financial participation is available; and a data-first verification duty. AB 2161 (Chapter 209) added a duty to let you confirm conflicting data before any adverse action, and chose the one-month lookback — the shortest federal law allows. California also funds its own state premium subsidy up to 200% FPL for 2027 ($300M). CA LegInfo · WIC § 14005.69CA LegInfo · AB 2161CA LegInfo · AB 2161Covered CA · Rates and Plans for 2027
What is the biggest risk? SSA §1902(xx)(7)(B): if you fail the requirement but would otherwise have been Medicaid-eligible, you are deemed eligible for minimum essential coverage and are therefore barred from the premium tax credit and its advance payments. DHCS tells members directly: “If you lose Medi-Cal because you did not meet these rules, you cannot get financial help to pay for a health plan through Covered California.” CMS/CMCS · Section 71119DHCS/MCED · MEDIL I 26-25
Does owning property disqualify you? No — not for the MAGI adult group. 42 CFR §435.603(g) says the agency “must not apply any assets or resources test”. The Medi-Cal asset limits DHCS describes ($130,000 now; $21,000 / $31,000 from July 1, 2027, counting “more than one house or vehicle”) apply to Non-MAGI groups — age 65+, disability, nursing home. See flagged item F-17 for why these are routinely confused. eCFR · 42 CFR 435.603DHCS · Medi-Cal Changes
Are you even in scope? Only if MAGI household income is at or below 138% FPL — $22,025/year ($1,836/month) for one person in 2026. Above that you are not in the adult group, the work requirement does not apply, and the route is a Covered California plan. Note the enhanced federal subsidy expired December 31, 2025, and the PTC repayment cap is removed from the 2026 tax year.
