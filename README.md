# MaltaProj9 — California healthcare eligibility research

A source-linked, static dashboard explaining California Medi-Cal and Covered California options for a U.S. citizen resident with no earned wages but possible rental, trading, and savings-interest income. It summarizes the federal Medicaid community-engagement law scheduled for 2027, California implementation materials, application routes, income treatment, and unresolved items.

## Research and limitations

Research was checked against official federal and California sources on **October 18, 2026**. Each factual table row includes an official source link. Unknown personal figures and unresolved classifications are intentionally blank or flagged, especially Kalshi contract tax treatment, how specific rental/interest amounts fit the separate 2027 gross-income test, and whether particular property-management/trading hours qualify. This is an informational research brief, not a personal eligibility decision or legal/tax advice. See [Flagged for Review](flagged.html) and [Sources & audit](sources.html).
