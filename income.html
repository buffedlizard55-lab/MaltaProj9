03 · Income classification

Separate the money test from the work test

Your income matters twice: for Medi-Cal/Covered California eligibility (MAGI—monthly for Medi-Cal, estimated-annual for marketplace credits) and for the 2027 community-engagement test (MAGI-based monthly income of $580 in a qualifying month). An account balance, interest on that account, property value, and net rental profit are not interchangeable—and none of your three streams is earned income, yet all of them count in MAGI.

No dollar amount or tax classification is inferred where the official sources do not establish one.
Known from official sources (re-verified 2026-10-18): HealthCare.gov’s MAGI guide includes expected interest, investment income, capital gains, and net rental income; self-employment is business income after expenses. DHCS says MAGI Medi-Cal does not apply property limits. Crucially, the 2027 work rule’s $580 path is measured with MAGI-based monthly income (42 CFR 435.552(f); Welf. & Inst. Code §14005.69(e)(6))—so the MAGI treatments below are the work-test treatments; DHCS’s earlier “gross income” phrasing was preliminary. IRS rules also confirm $0 earned income is coherent here: own-account trading gains are not self-employment earnings, and rental income generally is not either. What remains unestablished: the tax character of each Kalshi contract and the county’s month-by-month documentation method for trading P&L.

What “no earned income” does—and does not—tell you

For Medi-Cal income eligibility

  • Use expected household income for the coverage year, not only wages.
  • Include the required household members and their income under the application rules.
  • List net rent, interest, dividends, and taxable gains as the relevant official income guidance directs.
  • County determines Medi-Cal eligibility; use actual tax records and current estimates.

HealthCare.gov: how to count income

For the 2027 community-engagement test

  • One month of MAGI-based income ≥$580 from any source satisfies it—separate from 80 hours, and (at renewal) only one such month in the period is needed in California.
  • Compute that month with MAGI figures: net rent + interest + net realized trading gains. Medi-Cal budgets monthly; taxable lump sums count only in the month received.
  • Six-month averaging is federally for seasonal workers; do not assume variable trading gains can be averaged.
  • Confirm the county’s monthly P&L documentation method; never assume trading/management hours count.
