How to get covered in California

The most direct route for a low-income California resident is a Medi-Cal application. One California application screens for Medi-Cal and Covered California. You will need to provide accurate household and income information; the county or marketplace determines eligibility.

No personal financial details are needed for this guide. This site does not submit applications.
