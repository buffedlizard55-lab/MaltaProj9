(3) Was the exclusion list added by AB 2161? No — SB 164 added it and AB 2161 amended it, and the “23 categories” figure is a count of this project’s catalogue, not an official enumeration. Resolved in part 2026-10-18
$580 test is MAGI-based; monthly trading documentation + averaging scope remain open		Resolved: 42 CFR 435.552(f)(2) and Welf. & Inst. Code §14005.69(e)(6) measure the $580 test with MAGI-based income for the individual’s MAGI household—so net rental income, interest, and net capital/trading gains count (not gross rents/proceeds, not wages only). This supersedes DHCS’s preliminary “total gross income” wording; the DHCS member webpage’s narrower “work at a job and earn $580” phrasing still conflicts in wording. Still open: the county’s month-by-month method for realized Kalshi gains/losses, allocation of household MAGI to the individual, and whether 6-month averaging (federally for seasonal workers) covers merely fluctuating trading income as DHCS wording suggests.Use MAGI (net) figures and keep monthly records by source. Ask the county the three documentation/averaging/hours questions on the Income page before relying on averaging or on trading/management hours.42 CFR 435.552(f)–(g); AB 2161; DHCS member notice
Activity definition
Trading and own-property-management hours		Federal “work” covers paid/in-kind/unpaid work (42 CFR 435.552(b)), with self-employment included per the CMS preamble and DHCS guidance; no official source discusses trading one’s own account or managing one’s own rentals. IRS law says those profits are not self-employment earnings (Topic 429; Schedule E instructions), which supports $0 earned income but does not define Medicaid work. Paid management of others’ property is different: earned business income.Hours classification and sufficient documentation are unconfirmed—values left blank. Get written county confirmation before relying on such hours; the $580 MAGI month, structured community service, work program, half-time school, and exemptions are verified alternatives.42 CFR 435.552(b); IRS Topic 429; IRS Schedule E instructions
