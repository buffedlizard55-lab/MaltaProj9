What the 2027 Medicaid work rule actually requires

The law calls this a “community engagement requirement.” It is not a universal requirement that every Medi-Cal member hold a wage-paying job. An income pathway, school, qualifying work, volunteering, a work program, combinations, and exemptions are all part of the rules.

Federal law enacted July 4, 2025 · California says implementation begins January 1, 2027 · Guidance can evolve.
For your no-earned-income situation (re-verified 2026-10-18 against the federal regulation and California statute): the income path is MAGI-based monthly income of at least $580 from any source in a qualifying month (42 CFR 435.552(f); Welf. & Inst. Code §14005.69(e)(6))—net rent, interest, and net trading gains count on a MAGI basis; wages are not required. California needs that showing for any one month per renewal period (new applicants: the month before applying). DHCS’s earlier “total gross income” / fluctuating-income-averaging wording was preliminary; federal six-month averaging is limited to seasonal workers. If no month clears $580, use 80 hours of qualifying work, structured community service or a work program, at least half-time education, a combination, or an exemption. No official source decides whether your trading hours or own-property-management hours count as work. Losing Medi-Cal for noncompliance brings no Covered California financial help. Practical interpretation—not an eligibility decision

If a month’s MAGI-based non-wage income is ≥$580

That month satisfies the work rule by itself: at renewal only one such month in the period is needed in California. Keep monthly records for net rent (rents minus expenses), interest, and realized net trading gains, and confirm the county’s monthly documentation method. Separately, you must still meet Medi-Cal’s MAGI eligibility test (monthly budgeting; taxable lump sums count only in the month received).

Use MAGI (net) figures, not gross rent or gross trade proceeds—a depreciation-heavy rental month or net trading-loss month may fall short even with large gross receipts.

If every month is below $580 and no exemption applies

Use 80 hours in a qualifying month of structured community service, a work program, at least half-time school, or a combination. Full-time trading and self-managing your own rentals are not officially confirmed as qualifying work; obtain written county guidance before relying on those hours.

Do not misstate hours or income. Preserve verifiable records and follow the county notice process.

Timing irregularity: DHCS’s main policy tracker and ACWDL identify Jan. 1, 2027 as the start date, while a September 2026 member outreach letter says the rules “will not go into effect until March 2027” in one passage, then elsewhere says the member must start in 2027. The report does not silently reconcile this discrepancy. See Flagged for Review.
