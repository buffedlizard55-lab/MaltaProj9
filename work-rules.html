What the 2027 Medicaid work rule actually requires

The law calls this a “community engagement requirement.” It is not a universal requirement that every Medi-Cal member hold a wage-paying job. An income pathway, school, qualifying work, volunteering, a work program, combinations, and exemptions are all part of the rules.

Federal law enacted July 4, 2025 · California says implementation begins January 1, 2027 · Guidance can evolve.
For your no-earned-income situation: DHCS’s county guidance says the income path is total gross income of at least $580 in a month, “from any source”; for fluctuating income it describes a six-month average, with dividends among examples. That is a separate compliance route and is not limited to wages. If you are below that threshold, other qualifying routes include 80 hours of qualifying work (including self-employment), community service/volunteering or a work program, part-time education, or an exemption. Published sources reviewed do not conclusively classify Kalshi activity, your particular rent/interest, or your property-management hours for this test. See those open questions.

Practical interpretation—not an eligibility decision

If your monthly non-wage income is ≥$580

California DHCS describes a monthly gross income from any source path. Keep the records used for your reported rental, interest and trading income and ask the county how it will count each source under the work-rule test. The work test does not replace the separate MAGI income eligibility calculation.

Official wording is broader than wages; application to these specific income items is not individually spelled out in the sources reviewed.

If it is below $580 and you have no qualifying hours

You could meet an exemption if one actually applies, or use community service, qualifying job training, education, or combined activities. Full-time trading by itself is not officially identified as an approved work program or as qualifying self-employment; obtain county guidance before relying on it.

Do not misstate hours or income. Preserve verifiable records and follow the county notice process.

Timing irregularity: DHCS’s main policy tracker and ACWDL identify Jan. 1, 2027 as the start date, while a September 2026 member outreach letter says the rules “will not go into effect until March 2027” in one passage, then elsewhere says the member must start in 2027. The report does not silently reconcile this discrepancy. See Flagged for Review.
